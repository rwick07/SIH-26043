# SIH-26043
A comprehensive Societal Innovation Collaboration Portal

An AI-powered digital platform that transforms citizen-reported societal challenges into structured and actionable problems. Citizens can submit problems with descriptions, locations, and images, while AI analyzes, categorizes, prioritizes, and identifies related or duplicate reports. The platform provides a common ecosystem connecting government, universities, and industries, enabling relevant stakeholders to collaborate on suitable challenges, develop solutions, and track their progress toward real-world implementation.
